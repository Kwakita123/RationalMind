EMOTIONAL AWARENESS

Pause before you respond.

This exercise helps you separate what happened, what you felt, and what you believe happened. You don't need to eliminate emotion. You need to understand it.

01

What happened?

Describe the situation as objectively as possible. Focus on observable events rather than interpretations.

02

What are you feeling?

Name the emotion rather than judging it.

5
03

What thought is behind the emotion?

What are you telling yourself about the situation?

04

What evidence supports that thought?

Look for specific facts rather than assumptions.

05

What evidence challenges it?

Consider information that doesn't fit your original interpretation.

06

What else could be true?

Generate at least one alternative explanation.

07

What response aligns with your goals?

You may not control the situation, but you can choose your next action.

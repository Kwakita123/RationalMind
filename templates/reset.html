STRESS RESET

Pause. Breathe. Reset.

When stress or strong emotions make it difficult to think clearly, sometimes the best first step is simply to pause.

Reset is designed to give you a short moment to slow down, regain perspective, and return to the situation with a clearer mind.

Why is there no submission?

Reset is different from RationalMind's other tools. The purpose is not to analyze what happened or create a record of your experience.

When you're stressed, you may not need another task to complete. You may simply need a moment to step away, breathe, and regain control of your attention.

That's why Reset does not require you to submit anything or save your responses. You can use the exercises, finish when you feel ready, and move on with your day.

STEP 01

Identify what you can control

Stress can become stronger when we focus on things outside our control.

Ask yourself:

What part of this situation can I actually influence right now?

STEP 02

Take a moment to breathe

Give yourself a short break from the situation and focus your attention on your breathing.

Ready?

STEP 03

Ground yourself

Bring your attention back to the present moment instead of staying focused on the stressful situation.

Look around you.

Notice what you can see, hear, and physically feel around you.

1

Notice something around you.

Take your time. There is no need to submit anything.

STEP 04

Separate yourself from the reaction

You don't have to immediately act on every thought or emotion you experience.

Ask yourself:

What am I feeling right now, and do I need to act on it immediately?

STEP 05

Decide what comes next

After creating some space, ask yourself what the most useful next step is.

Remember:

You don't need to solve everything right now. Focus on the next reasonable step.

Reset, don't react.

The goal isn't to eliminate emotions or pretend that stressful situations don't matter. The goal is to create enough space between the emotion and your response to choose what you want to do next.

Nothing needs to be submitted. Nothing needs to be saved. When you're ready, simply leave the Reset tool and continue your day.

THE RATIONALMIND FRAMEWORK

From reaction to reflection.

Human decisions are influenced by emotions, thoughts, and assumptions. RationalMind helps create awareness between experiencing something and responding.

1. Awareness

Recognize your emotions and current state.

2. Examination

Analyze thoughts, assumptions, and evidence.

3. Choice

Make decisions aligned with long-term goals.

4. Growth

Learn from experiences and improve future responses.

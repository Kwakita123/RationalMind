THE RATIONALMIND APPROACH

Think clearly. Understand yourself. Choose intentionally.

RationalMind is designed to help you examine your experiences, understand your emotional responses, and approach important decisions with greater awareness.

Reflect

Examine what happened, what you felt, and how you interpreted the situation.

Reason

Separate observations from assumptions and consider whether your conclusions are supported by evidence.

Decide

Consider your goals, alternatives, tradeoffs, and possible consequences before choosing.

RationalMind is not about eliminating emotion. It is about creating enough distance to understand emotion before deciding how to respond.

YOUR PRIVACY

Your personal reflections stay personal.

The personal information you enter into RationalMind, including your reflections, thoughts, decisions, history, and written responses, is not displayed to the creator.

The creator dashboard uses limited aggregate information, such as anonymous emotional patterns and intensity data, to understand how RationalMind is being used.

Your individual reflection content is not used as creator analytics.

RATIONALMIND PRINCIPLE

You cannot control every event.

But you can improve how you understand, evaluate, and respond to those events.

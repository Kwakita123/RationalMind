Privacy & Interpretation

Creator analytics use aggregate, self-reported information.

Usernames and email addresses are not displayed in this dashboard, and individual reflection content is not presented as creator analytics.

Emotional measurements represent users' self-reported experiences and should not be interpreted as clinical measurements.

Research limitation: These results describe patterns in self-reported data. They do not establish that RationalMind caused the reported changes.

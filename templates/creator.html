Product Growth Insights

The Creator Dashboard can be used to identify broad patterns that may help guide future improvements to RationalMind.

For example, repeated emotional transitions, frequently reported starting states, feedback ratings, and user comments can help identify which parts of the experience users find useful and where the app may need improvement.

These signals should be evaluated alongside additional data as the number of users grows.

How the Analysis Works

Negative emotions: movement toward zero is interpreted as a reduction in reported intensity.

Positive emotions: an increase in reported positive intensity is interpreted as movement in the intended direction.

Improved: the reported intensity moved in the intended direction.

No meaningful change: the reported intensity remained the same.

Worsened: the reported intensity moved in the opposite direction.

Only reflections with corresponding feedback and valid before-and-after intensity values are included in before-and-after comparisons.

Privacy & Data Protection

Creator analytics are designed around aggregate information rather than individual user monitoring.

Usernames and email addresses are not displayed in this dashboard.

Individual reflection content is not displayed as part of the analytics.

Emotional measurements and feedback are used to understand aggregate patterns in the RationalMind experience.

Important: RationalMind analytics represent self-reported experiences. They describe patterns in the collected data and do not establish that RationalMind caused the reported changes. Small datasets may produce unstable patterns, so conclusions should become stronger only as more data is collected.
